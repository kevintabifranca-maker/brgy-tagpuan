History, Creation & Culture

Origin & Etymology

The name Tagpu-an originates from the Hiligaynon word "tagpo" or "tagpu-an", which translates to "a place of meeting" or "meeting point". Historically, this area served as a strategic rendezvous point for early settlers, traders, and neighboring mountain communities traveling across the scenic terrain of Tubungan.

How It Was Created

In the past, Tagpu-an was a serene rural settlement reliant on subsistence farming and upland agriculture. As Tubungan grew into an established municipality in Iloilo, Tagpu-an was formally delineated into an official barangay under municipal local government administration, fostering structured community governance, local infrastructure, and public services.

Culture & Traditions

Deeply rooted in traditional Hiligaynon heritage, Tagpu-an thrives on the spirit of Bayanihan (mutual unity). The community celebrates annual religious and agricultural fiestas, honoring patron saints with customary feasts, gatherings, and thanksgiving for peaceful harvests and mountain agriculture.

Our Vision

To be a peaceful, self-reliant, and ecologically sustainable barangay in Tubungan, guided by transparent governance and an empowered, united community.

Our Mission

To deliver efficient public services, preserve our cultural heritage, promote sustainable agricultural livelihoods, and safeguard the well-being of every resident in Barangay Tagpu-an.
